    ABOUT RI-SWAP

    Community exchange on RI-YELLOW

    RI-SWAP helps businesses and people find reciprocal needs, negotiate private terms, and record a reviewable Agreement. The same model supports B2B, B2C, C2B, and C2C; each party still reviews and decides.

    The current scope ends at Agreement Reached after exactly two parties accept the same version and content. It does not process payment or prove delivery, quality, or fulfilment; unavailable connections remain fail-closed.

    How to complete an exchange Sign-in + four steps

    At sign-in, only a provider marked available can be used; options still being configured or unavailable remain locked. RI-YELLOW OTP verifies only the enrolled contact channel, not business ownership.

    1. Offer — state what you give, what you need, the location, availability, and package count.
    2. Discover — find a suitable Offer and select the reciprocal need.
    3. Proposal — each counterparty negotiates on a separate Proposal; Offer capacity is still enforced.
    4. Accept — exactly two parties must accept the same proposal version and content before the system creates an Agreement.

    Agreement Reached records an agreement; it is not payment, delivery, fulfilment, or a legal guarantee.
